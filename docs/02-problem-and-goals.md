# 02 – Problem & Goals

## Current Pain Points (Shared Fleet Context)

1. **Instrument data is hard to see**  
   Many club boats have instruments at the helm or scattered. Cabin visibility is often poor, especially at night or in rain.

2. **Check-in / check-out is informal**  
   Paper logs or verbal handovers make it harder to track who used a boat, when, and whether any issues were noted.

3. **Maintenance feedback is delayed**  
   Problems discovered on a sail are sometimes not reported promptly or clearly.

4. **Phones are already the main interface**  
   Members use phones for weather, booking, photos, and messaging. Fighting this reality is less effective than working with it.

5. **Commercial solutions are expensive**  
   Equipping five boats with modern MFDs would cost many thousands of dollars and lock the club into proprietary ecosystems.

## Project Goals

### Primary Goals

- Provide a clear, always-available local display of key boat data in the cabin
- Make check-in and check-out simple, digital, and auditable
- Allow members to report issues and log hours easily
- Keep total cost per boat low enough that the club can equip the whole fleet
- Remain maintainable by club volunteers using open tools

### Secondary Goals

- Support future enhancements (engine-hour alerts, simple checklists, visitor information)
- Integrate cleanly with the existing LMYC booking system
- Work offline for core instrument display (cellular only needed for pairing / logging)

### Non-Goals (Explicitly Out of Scope for v1)

- Full chartplotting or route planning on the terminal
- Autopilot control
- Replacing the official logbook or legal requirements
- Real-time tracking of boats by the club while under way (privacy)
- High-brightness outdoor / helm displays (cabin focus first)

## Success Criteria for a Pilot

A successful pilot on one boat would demonstrate:

- Reliable display of live NMEA data
- Smooth phone pairing and un-pairing tied to bookings
- Members can complete a check-in → sail → check-out cycle without frustration
- At least a few useful issue reports or hour logs captured digitally
- Hardware survives normal cabin conditions for several months
